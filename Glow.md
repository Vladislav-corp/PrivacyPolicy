## Privacy Policy for Glow

**Effective date:** May 2026

Glow is an iOS application that turns your iPhone in StandBy mode into a customizable bedside clock with a catalog of themes.

### Data Collection

Glow does **not collect, store, process, or share any personal data**.

The app:

* does not require user accounts
* does not use analytics services
* does not track usage
* does not use third-party SDKs
* does not transmit any data to external servers

All functionality runs locally on your device. User preferences (selected theme, settings) are stored in the on-device App Group container and are never transmitted off-device.

### Location

Some themes (Tide, Sunrise) may request access to your approximate location to display location-dependent content (e.g., next tide event, sunrise time). Location data is used **only on-device** to compute the display and is not stored, logged, or transmitted to any server.

You can deny or revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Glow. Themes that depend on location will fall back to a static state.

### Calendar

The Calendar Next theme reads upcoming events from your iOS calendar via EventKit to display the next event and a countdown. Calendar data is read **only on-device** and is not stored, logged, or transmitted off-device.

You can deny or revoke calendar access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars → Glow.

### Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple via the App Store. Apple's privacy policy governs how Apple handles purchase data. Glow itself receives only the entitlement status (whether a theme pack is unlocked) from StoreKit and stores it in the on-device App Group container.

### Widgets and Live Activities

Glow widgets and Live Activities run on-device using WidgetKit and ActivityKit. They read the same local preferences and theme assets as the main app. No data is transmitted off-device.

### Children

Glow is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

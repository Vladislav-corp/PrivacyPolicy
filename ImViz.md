## Privacy Policy for ImViz

**Effective date:** August 2026

ImViz is an iOS utility that extracts text from photos, PDFs, and text files (OCR) and lets you copy or share the result.

### Data Collection

ImViz does **not collect, store, process, or share any personal data**.

The app:

* does not require user accounts
* does not use analytics services
* does not track usage
* does not use third-party SDKs
* does not transmit any data to external servers

All functionality runs locally on your device.

### Photos and Files

ImViz uses the system photo picker and the system file picker, so the app receives only the specific items you choose. It never gets access to your full photo library or file system.

Text recognition is performed entirely on-device with Apple's Vision framework. Recognized text is kept in memory for the duration of the session, and the file you export is written to the app's temporary folder inside its sandbox so you can share it. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

### On-Device Storage

The app stores a few counters on your device: how many documents you have processed under the free tier, how many recognitions have completed successfully (used only to decide when to show the system review prompt), and whether the ImViz Pro upgrade has been purchased. These values stay on the device and are not linked to your identity.

### In-App Purchases

The optional ImViz Pro upgrade is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store.
ImViz does not receive or store any payment information or personal details related to the purchase.

### Changes

If this policy changes in the future, updates will be posted on this page.

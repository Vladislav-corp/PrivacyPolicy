## Privacy Policy for Uni

**Effective date:** July 2026

Uni is a macOS utility for uninstalling applications and cleaning up disk space (caches, logs, duplicates, large and old files).

### Data Collection

Uni does **not collect, store, process, or share any personal data**.

The app:

* does not require user accounts
* does not use analytics services
* does not track usage
* does not use third-party SDKs
* does not transmit any data to external servers

All functionality runs locally on your device.

### File Access

Uni accesses only the folders you explicitly grant access to through the system file picker.
Files are analyzed locally to show sizes, dates, and duplicates. Deleted items are moved to the Trash.
No file contents or metadata are sent outside your device.

### In-App Purchases

The optional Uni Pro upgrade is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store.
Uni does not receive or store any payment information or personal details related to the purchase.

### Changes

If this policy changes in the future, updates will be posted on this page.
